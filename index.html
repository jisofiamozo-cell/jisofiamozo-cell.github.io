THE ISLAND GEM

Siargao is an island in the province of Surigao del Norte, Philippines! Known for its world-class surf break “Cloud 9,” pristine lagoons, caves, and island-hopping.

Regional Travel & Logistics

Modes of Transport

Getting around Siargao involves air, ferry, and land travel. Most visitors enter directly through Sayak Airport (IAO).

  • To Siargao: Direct flights to IAO from Manila and Cebu.
  • Nearby Airport Option: Fly to Butuan (BXU), then travel to Surigao City and ferry to Dapa Port (Siargao).
  • On the Island: Motorcycle rentals, vans, habal-habal, and tricycles are the primary transport options.

Must-See Spots

Local Delicacies

Grilled Seafood

Siargao offers some of the freshest seafood in the country.

Kinilaw

A Filipino ceviche dish made with fresh tuna, vinegar, and spices.

Adobo

A classic Filipino dish enjoyed all throughout the island.

Hidden Gems

Sugba Lagoon

A stunning turquoise lagoon in Del Carmen, perfect for kayaking, paddleboarding, and diving.

Maasin River

A scenic jungle river famous for coconut-tree swinging and river paddling.

Magpupungko Rock Pools

Natural tidal pools that appear during low tide—one of Siargao’s most iconic spots.

Siargao's Culture

Surfing and Island Life

Surf culture defines Siargao's identity. From Cloud 9 surf breaks to laid-back beach living, the island embodies a unique blend of adventure and hospitality.

Major Festivals

  • Siargao International Surfing Cup: Held annually in Cloud 9.
  • Siargao Island Festival: Celebrates local culture, cuisine, and traditions.
  • Pili Festival: A community celebration centered around local produce.

Educational Institutions

Siargao Island Institute of Technology

A local institution offering education and skills training for youth.

Espoir School of Life

A non-profit organization providing free education in Barangay Lobogon, Del Carmen, Siargao.
